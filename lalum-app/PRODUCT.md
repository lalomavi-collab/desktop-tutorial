# Product: LALUM

## Who it is for

Owners, investors and decision makers who need a trusted legal and strategic advisor: people in Israel and abroad weighing a real estate deal, an urban renewal project, or the adoption of AI in legal and business work. They arrive worried about risk and leave when they believe the practice is exact, independent and senior.

## Register

Brand and product. The public site persuades and teaches. The portal and tools (`/portal`, `/os`, `/risk`) operate. Choose the mode per surface: the home page and focus areas persuade, articles and guides are read, the quiz and portal operate.

## Practice

LALUM is the practice of ד״ר עו״ד אברהם ללום (Dr. Avraham Lalum, Adv.). It leads with two areas only:

1. Legal AI: advisory, ongoing accompaniment and training.
2. Real estate and urban renewal, in Israel and abroad.

The operating model layered over them is Clinic (`/advisory`), Engine (`#engine` on the home page) and Decision Room (`/risk`). These are not extra areas. Mediation stays live but is not promoted.

The home page opens with Legal AI alone. Real estate appears lower on the page, never in the hero.

## Voice

Direct, exact, analytical, senior. No flattery, no hype, no filler. States the risk plainly and the alternative that produces the most value. Hebrew first, with the same quality in English and the other languages. Never uses dashes as punctuation.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS gradients, glow, neon, glassmorphism.
- Stock imagery of handshakes, gavels, scales.
- Dark "crypto" or "AI" aesthetics.
- Anything that reads as a law firm template from the 2010s.
- Any logo other than the supplied wordmark.

## Principles

1. Trust is built by precision: real figures, real sources, nothing unverified on the page.
2. Restraint over decoration. The existing ivory and sage system is the identity.
3. Hebrew right-to-left is the reference layout, not an afterthought.
4. Accessibility is a floor: contrast AA, visible focus, reduced motion respected.
5. One subject per change, no copy edits inside design work.

## Constraints

- Copy is shared across five languages and the prerender: design work does not change it.
- Logo: `Wordmark` component or the files in `brand/README.md` only.
- Rulings, statutes and amounts appear only after double verification (see root `CLAUDE.md`).
