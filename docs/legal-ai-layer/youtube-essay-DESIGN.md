# DESIGN: "Google Just Built an AI Lawyer's Assistant. Who Signs?"

Locked brand and language decisions for this film (the gates of the LALUM video workflow).

## Language
* Everything on screen and in the narration is English. Hebrew appears only in the verbatim court quotation (HCJ 38379-12-24, para 26), set right to left in its own card, with an unofficial English translation under it.
* Numbers and dates: Arabic numerals, day first (25.8.2026). Product names stay as the vendor writes them.

## Brand
* Logo: only the supplied wordmark artwork, `lalum-app/public/lalum-logo-inverse.svg`, never set in a typeface.
* Domain: **lalumapp.com** is the only domain on screen and in the description.
* Palette of the film (matches the site videos): deep green #0b100c to #1f3626, cream #f5f1e8, amber #e7c27a, soft green #9cc29f, panel green #16241b with border #3a5a42.
* Type: Liberation Sans (bold for headlines), Liberation Mono for the HUD. Headlines 72px or more, captions 52px, labels 22px or more.
* Name form: "Dr. Avraham Lalum, Adv." wherever the name appears (description only).

## Claims
* Every statement about the product is Google's own wording and is attributed (tag on screen). Our reading is labelled "Our analysis".
* No figures about time saved, revenue or hours. Verified record: `docs/legal-ai-layer/verified-claims.md`.
* Product windows are illustrative mockups and say so. LALUM is not affiliated with Google.

## Fixed outro
Wordmark, a call to action ("Want to stay one step ahead on AI and the law?"), **lalumapp.com**, held for more than 2.5 seconds, then rolling credits.

## Motion language
* One continuous shot: six rooms, a camera that travels along an amber thread (the signature line) and lands in the next room. Five match cuts.
* 84 BPM. Every scene starts on a downbeat. Kinetic words snap to a sixteenth grid.
* Cinematic finish: letterbox, teal and amber grade, grain, rough texture, dust, light leaks, spotlight.
