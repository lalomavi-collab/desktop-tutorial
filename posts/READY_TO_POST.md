### אנגלית (לינקדאין)

```
Case study: how a single AI shortcut erased 40% of a deal.

An Israeli tech company was weeks from an acquisition worth tens of millions. Then due diligence asked one question the founders had never considered:

How was your product built?

The answer: their flagship software had been improved with AI agents, and in the process, proprietary code and data had been fed into an external tool, flowing straight into the model vendor's training set.

The buyer saw it immediately. Not a bug to fix before closing, but something worse: a permanent loss of IP exclusivity.

▸ Data that trained a model cannot simply be deleted. It is absorbed.
▸ An asset that lost its exclusivity no longer commands a premium.
▸ The result: a sharp cut to the valuation, and founder earn-out incentives unwound.

The hard truth for founders: how your team uses AI in development is no longer a technical detail. It is part of your company's value on the day it matters most, at a raise or at an exit.

The hard truth for acquirers and investors: due diligence that ignores AI leakage misses the difference between a unique asset and one that quietly lost its edge.

The good news is that this is preventable, and cheaply: an AI use policy, tools contractually bound not to train on your input, and the right clauses with your model vendors.
```
